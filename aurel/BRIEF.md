# AUREL — Luxury Skincare Campaign
## Professional Editing & Post-Production Brief

ROLE:
Act as a senior commercial film editor, beauty cinematographer, colorist and sound designer.

OBJECTIVE:
Assemble the six supplied video clips into a cohesive, premium 30-second luxury skincare advertisement for the fictional brand AUREL.

The campaign must feel art-directed, elegant and photorealistic, with consistent product design and intentional visual storytelling.

### 1. Timeline

| Time | Scene | Editing direction |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:04 | The First Drop | Slow opening, subtle low-frequency atmosphere, delicate droplet sound |
| 00:04–00:08 | Skin in Light | Clean match cut, soft airy ambience |
| 00:08–00:12 | The Formula | Precisely timed liquid sound, restrained slow motion if appropriate |
| 00:12–00:17 | The Ritual | Natural pacing, intimate and subtle texture |
| 00:17–00:24 | The Reveal | Gradual musical rise, refined hero-product reveal |
| 00:24–00:30 | The Signature | Clean final packshot, logo reveal and musical resolution |

Use the strongest portions of each clip. Adjust in/out points to preserve natural movement and eliminate visual glitches. If source clips are too short or too long, adapt the cuts without introducing artificial scenes.

### 2. Editing Language

- Favor elegant straight cuts and motivated match cuts.
- Match the serum droplet highlight to the highlight on the woman's skin.
- Transition from the skin ritual to the product reveal using motion, shape or light.
- Avoid excessive dissolves, transitions, speed ramps and generic social-media effects.
- Do not reverse footage when doing so would make liquid or hand movements look unnatural.
- Preserve believable motion and natural timing.
- Use subtle stabilization only if needed.

### 3. Product Continuity

The AUREL bottle is a fixed brand asset.

Do not change its silhouette, dimensions, cap, glass tint, label position, label proportions or physical appearance.

Do not invent new packaging, alternate labels, additional bottles or branding. Use the original product-reference image to guide continuity wherever applicable.

If an AI-generated clip contains an inconsistent bottle, prioritize the cleanest correct shot. Recommend replacing the shot rather than hiding the inconsistency with excessive effects.

### 4. Color Grading

Develop a cohesive luxury beauty grade across all six scenes.

- Warm ivory highlights.
- Restrained champagne reflections.
- Natural, believable skin tones.
- Rich but detailed espresso shadows in the opening.
- Soft contrast and controlled highlight roll-off.
- Neutral whites without excessive yellow.
- No oversaturation, plastic skin, artificial glow or crushed shadows.

The opening may be darker, but the overall campaign should transition naturally toward the bright ivory final packshot.

### 5. Sound Design

Build an original or properly licensed minimal beauty-advertising soundtrack.

Music:
- Soft atmospheric pads.
- Sparse, delicate piano notes.
- Restrained organic textures.
- A gradual rise during the product reveal.
- A clean and memorable final resolution.

Sound effects:
- Subtle glass or liquid detail in the opening.
- Delicate water-drop sound synchronized with the serum droplet.
- Gentle airy texture during the macro skin shot.
- Quiet, intimate ambience during the application scene.
- A subtle low-impact musical accent during the hero reveal.

Keep all effects tasteful and realistic. Avoid exaggerated splashes, cinematic booms, loud whooshes or distracting sound design.

Use licensed or original audio. Do not imitate a specific copyrighted soundtrack.

### 6. Typography and Branding

Add branding only in post-production.

Brand: AUREL
Product: RADIANCE SERUM
Tagline: Your Skin, In Its Own Light.

Use elegant, restrained typography with generous spacing. Keep the final product unobstructed.

Suggested final reveal:
- Brand name appears above the bottle.
- Tagline appears below it.
- Use a simple, subtle opacity fade.
- Do not animate individual letters dramatically.
- Ensure all text remains inside platform-safe margins.

Do not add unsupported cosmetic claims, clinical results, before-and-after claims or invented ingredients.

### 7. Deliverables

Primary:
- 1080 × 1920 vertical master.
- 9:16 aspect ratio.
- Approximately 30 seconds.
- High-quality H.264 MP4.
- 24 or 25 fps, consistent with the selected source footage.
- AAC stereo audio, 48 kHz.
- Consistent exposure and color across scenes.

Website adaptation:
- 1920 × 1080, 16:9.
- Reframe from the best original footage, not by stretching the vertical export.
- Preserve product visibility and typography.
- Produce a separate edit if necessary.

Also prepare:
- A clean version without text or logos.
- A final packshot still.
- A short editing report describing any continuity issues.

If direct rendering is unavailable, produce an implementation-ready timeline, exact clip trims, audio cue placements, color-grading recommendations and a runnable editing script or project workflow supported by the available environment.

FINAL QUALITY STANDARD:
The result should resemble a professionally art-directed luxury skincare campaign: restrained, tactile, photorealistic, visually consistent and memorable. Every shot must serve the same brand identity.
